# Type 2 Diabetes Management

## Summary
Type 2 diabetes is a chronic condition that affects the way the body processes blood sugar (glucose). In type 2 diabetes, the body either doesn't produce enough insulin or resists its effects.

## Explanation
Insulin is a hormone that regulates the movement of sugar into your cells. With type 2 diabetes, your cells don't respond normally to insulin (insulin resistance). As a result, sugar builds up in your bloodstream. Over time, high blood sugar levels can lead to disorders of the circulatory, nervous, and immune systems.

## Lifestyle Suggestions
- **Blood Sugar Monitoring**: Track blood glucose levels regularly as recommended by your doctor.
- **Healthy Eating**: Focus on high-fiber foods, whole grains, fruits, vegetables, and lean proteins. Monitor carbohydrate intake.
- **Regular Activity**: Aim for at least 30 minutes of moderate physical activity, like walking, daily.
- **Weight Management**: Losing a modest amount of weight can help improve insulin sensitivity.

## Emergency Warning Signs
Seek emergency medical care if you experience symptoms of diabetic ketoacidosis (DKA) or hyperosmolar state:
- Extreme thirst and frequent urination.
- Confusion, drowsiness, or difficulty breathing.
- Fruity-smelling breath.
- Blood sugar levels consistently above 240 mg/dL with ketones in the urine.
