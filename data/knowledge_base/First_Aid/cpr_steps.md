# CPR (Cardiopulmonary Resuscitation)

## Summary
Cardiopulmonary resuscitation (CPR) is an emergency procedure that can help save a person's life if their breathing or heart has stopped.

## Explanation
When a person's heart stops beating, they are in cardiac arrest. CPR uses chest compressions and rescue breaths to mimic the heart's pumping action and circulate oxygenated blood to the brain and other vital organs.

## Steps for Hands-Only CPR
1. **Call 911**: Ensure the scene is safe, tap the person's shoulder, and ask if they are okay. If they do not respond, call 911 or ask someone else to call, and get an AED if available.
2. **Position**: Place the heel of one hand in the center of the person's chest, and place your other hand on top, interlocking your fingers.
3. **Compressions**: Push hard and fast in the center of the chest (100 to 120 compressions per minute). Allow the chest to rise completely between compressions.
4. **Continue**: Keep performing compressions until emergency medical help arrives or an AED becomes available.
