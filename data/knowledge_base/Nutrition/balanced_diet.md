# Nutrition and Balanced Diet

## Summary
A balanced diet provides your body with the essential nutrients, vitamins, and minerals it needs to function correctly.

## Explanation
To get the proper nutrition from your diet, you should consume most of your daily calories from:
- Fresh fruits and vegetables.
- Whole grains.
- Lean proteins (poultry, fish, beans, legumes).
- Healthy fats (olive oil, avocados, nuts).
- Low-fat dairy products.

## Lifestyle Suggestions
- **Hydration**: Drink 2 to 3 liters of water daily.
- **Portion Control**: Pay attention to portion sizes to avoid overeating.
- **Limit Processed Foods**: Reduce intake of added sugars, saturated fats, and high-sodium foods.
- **Meal Planning**: Plan meals ahead to ensure a diverse intake of nutrients.
