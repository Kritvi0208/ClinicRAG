# First Trimester of Pregnancy

## Summary
The first trimester of pregnancy begins on the first day of your last period and lasts until the end of week 12. This is a period of rapid development for the fetus and significant hormonal changes for the mother.

## Explanation
During the first trimester, the body goes through many changes. Hormones affect almost every organ system. Symptoms such as extreme fatigue, morning sickness (nausea and vomiting), mood swings, and frequent urination are very common.

## Home Care
- **Prenatal Vitamins**: Take a daily prenatal vitamin containing folic acid to support neural tube development.
- **Diet**: Eat small, frequent meals to combat nausea. Avoid raw fish, unpasteurized dairy, and undercooked meat.
- **Rest**: Sleep when you feel tired, as your body is working hard to build the placenta.
- **Hydration**: Drink plenty of water throughout the day.

## Emergency Warning Signs
Seek immediate emergency medical evaluation if you experience:
- Severe vaginal bleeding.
- Severe abdominal pain or cramping.
- Persistent vomiting that prevents keeping any fluids down for 24 hours.
- High fever or chills.
