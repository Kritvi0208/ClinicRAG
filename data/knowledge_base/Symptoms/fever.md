# Fever and Body Temperature

## Summary
Fever is a temporary increase in body temperature, often due to an illness. It is a sign that your body's immune system is fighting off an infection.

## Explanation
A normal body temperature is typically around 98.6°F (37°C), but it can vary slightly. A fever is generally defined as a body temperature of 100.4°F (38°C) or higher. Common causes include viral or bacterial infections, heat exhaustion, and certain inflammatory conditions.

## Home Care
- **Hydration**: Drink plenty of fluids (water, clear broths, herbal teas).
- **Rest**: Allow your body to rest to help fight the infection.
- **Cool environment**: Wear lightweight clothing and keep the room temperature comfortable.
- **Medications**: Acetaminophen (paracetamol) or ibuprofen can help reduce fever, but should be used according to package directions.

## Emergency Warning Signs
Seek immediate medical attention if a fever is accompanied by:
- Stiff neck or severe headache.
- Difficulty breathing or chest pain.
- Confusion, extreme drowsiness, or seizures.
- A fever in an infant under 3 months old (temperature of 100.4°F/38°C or higher).
