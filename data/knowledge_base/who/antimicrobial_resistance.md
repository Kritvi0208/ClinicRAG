# WHO Antimicrobial Resistance Guidelines

## Summary
Antimicrobial resistance (AMR) threatens the effective prevention and treatment of an ever-increasing range of infections.

## Key Recommendations
- **Prescription Stewardship**: Only use antibiotics when prescribed by a certified health professional.
- **Completion**: Always complete the full course of treatment, even if feeling better.
- **Infection Prevention**: Prevent infections by regularly washing hands, preparing food hygienically, and keeping vaccinations up to date.
