# WHO Asthma Guideline

## Summary
Asthma is a major non-communicable disease affecting both children and adults. WHO coordinates with GINA to define stepwise chronic care protocols.

## Key Recommendations
- **Reliever**: Low-dose inhaled corticosteroid (ICS)-formoterol is preferred as reliever therapy.
- **Controller**: Avoid using short-acting beta2-agonists (SABA) alone without ICS due to severe exacerbation risks.
- **Triggers**: Limit exposure to smoke, allergens, and occupational sensitizers.
