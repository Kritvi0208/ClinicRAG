# WHO Breastfeeding Guideline

## Summary
Breastfeeding is one of the most effective ways to ensure child health and survival. Guidelines support exclusive early feeding.

## Key Recommendations
- **Early Initiation**: Initiate breastfeeding within the first hour of birth.
- **Exclusivity**: Exclusive breastfeeding for the first 6 months of life. No water, formula, or other liquids.
- **Duration**: Continuous breastfeeding up to 2 years of age or beyond, with appropriate complementary foods starting at 6 months.
