# WHO Dengue Guideline

## Summary
Dengue is a viral infection transmitted by Aedes mosquitoes. Management hinges on fluid monitoring to prevent shock.

## Key Recommendations
- **Warning Signs**: Severe abdominal pain, persistent vomiting, fluid accumulation, mucosal bleeding, lethargy.
- **Care**: Oral rehydration therapy for mild cases; strict intravenous crystalloid management for severe plasma leakage.
- **Avoidance**: Do not prescribe aspirin or NSAIDs (like ibuprofen) due to increased hemorrhage risk.
