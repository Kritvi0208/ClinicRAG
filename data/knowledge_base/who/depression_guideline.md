# WHO Depression Guideline

## Summary
Depression is characterized by persistent sadness and lack of interest in activities. mhGAP defines clear steps for care.

## Key Recommendations
- **First-Line**: Psychoeducation, cognitive behavioral therapy (CBT), or interpersonal therapy (IPT).
- **Pharmacotherapy**: Moderate-to-severe cases can be managed with SSRIs (e.g. Fluoxetine) under professional supervision.
- **Duration**: Continue antidepressant treatment for at least 9-12 months after remission.
