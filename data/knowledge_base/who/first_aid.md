# WHO First Aid Guidelines

## Summary
First aid refers to the immediate care provided to a sick or injured person. WHO guidelines cover basic community response principles.

## Key Recommendations
- **CPR**: 30 chest compressions to 2 rescue breaths at a speed of 100-120 compressions per minute.
- **Bleeding Control**: Direct pressure using clean dressings, elevate limb if safe. Avoid routine tourniquet use unless catastrophic.
- **Burns**: Flush immediately with cool running water. Do not apply home remedies like oil or grease.
