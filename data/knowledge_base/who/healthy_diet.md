# WHO Healthy Diet Guideline

## Summary
A healthy diet helps to protect against malnutrition in all its forms, as well as NCDs including diabetes, heart disease.

## Key Recommendations
- **Fruits & Veg**: Eat at least 400g (5 portions) of fruit and vegetables per day, excluding potatoes.
- **Free Sugars**: Reduce free sugars intake to less than 10% (ideally under 5%) of total energy intake.
- **Dietary Diversity**: Ensure a diverse intake of whole grains, nuts, and healthy unsaturated fats.
