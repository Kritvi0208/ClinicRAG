# WHO Hypertension Guideline

## Summary
Hypertension is a critical contributor to cardiovascular disease. The WHO guidelines provide standards for pharmacological treatment threshold and target pressure controls.

## Key Recommendations
- **Initiation**: Start pharmacological treatment when blood pressure is >= 140/90 mmHg in general adults.
- **BP Targets**: Aim for a target pressure of < 130/80 mmHg in high-risk patients (e.g. those with diabetes).
- **Therapy**: Combination of ACE inhibitors, ARBs, calcium channel blockers, or thiazide-like diuretics.
