# WHO Mental Health Guideline

## Summary
The WHO mhGAP program aims to scale up services for mental, neurological, and substance use disorders.

## Key Recommendations
- **Primary Care**: Integrate mental health checkups in regular primary care visits.
- **Support**: Deploy psychological first aid and brief counseling protocols (CBT).
- **Safety**: Address acute self-harm risk immediately by removing lethal means and providing continuous supervision.
