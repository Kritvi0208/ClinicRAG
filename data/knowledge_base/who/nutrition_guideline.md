# WHO Nutrition Guideline

## Summary
Proper nutrition throughout the life course prevents all forms of malnutrition as well as non-communicable diseases.

## Key Recommendations
- **Fats**: Saturated fats should constitute less than 10% of total energy intake, and trans-fats less than 1%.
- **Sodium**: Limit salt intake to less than 5 grams per day for adults (equivalent to under 2,000 mg sodium).
- **Micronutrients**: Standardize supplementation of iron and folic acid for pregnant women.
