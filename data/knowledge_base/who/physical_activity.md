# WHO Physical Activity Guideline

## Summary
Physical activity benefits heart, body, and mind. WHO recommendations target all ages to reduce sedentary behavior.

## Key Recommendations
- **Adults**: Aim for 150-300 minutes of moderate-intensity, or 75-150 minutes of vigorous-intensity aerobic physical activity weekly.
- **Muscle Strength**: Perform muscle-strengthening activities involving all major muscle groups on 2 or more days a week.
- **Sedentary time**: Limit sedentary time and replace with light activity of any intensity.
