# WHO Pneumonia Guideline

## Summary
Pneumonia is a leading infectious cause of mortality among children worldwide. WHO IMCI protocols guide management.

## Key Recommendations
- **Classification**: Distinguish between 'Pneumonia' (fast breathing) and 'Severe Pneumonia' (chest indrawing/danger signs).
- **Antibiotic First-Line**: Oral amoxicillin for home care of non-severe cases.
- **Severe Cases**: Referral to hospital for IV ampicillin/gentamicin and oxygen therapy.
