# WHO Pregnancy Guideline

## Summary
A positive pregnancy experience requires high-quality antenatal care (ANC). Guidelines define diagnostic and support steps.

## Key Recommendations
- **ANC Contacts**: Minimum of 8 antenatal care contacts to reduce perinatal mortality and improve care quality.
- **Supplements**: Daily oral iron (30-60mg) and folic acid (400mcg) supplementation.
- **Screening**: Standard screening for gestational diabetes, anemia, and infectious diseases (HIV, Syphilis, Hep B).
