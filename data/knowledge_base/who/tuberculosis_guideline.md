# WHO Tuberculosis Guideline

## Summary
Tuberculosis (TB) remains a global threat. Guidelines focus on rapid diagnostic genotyping and shortened treatment courses.

## Key Recommendations
- **Diagnosis**: Use rapid molecular tests (e.g., GeneXpert) rather than smear microscopy as the initial test.
- **Treatment**: Standard 6-month regimen for drug-susceptible TB (isoniazid, rifampicin, pyrazinamide, ethambutol).
- **Preventative Care**: TB preventative treatment (TPT) for household contacts and people living with HIV.
