# WHO Vaccination Guideline

## Summary
Immunization prevents millions of deaths every year. WHO outlines standard schedule parameters for infants.

## Key Recommendations
- **At Birth**: BCG (Tuberculosis), Hep B, and Oral Polio Vaccine (OPV-0).
- **Infancy Routine**: DTP, Haemophilus influenzae type b, Pneumococcal, Rotavirus, and Measles-Rubella vaccines.
- **Cold Chain**: Strict maintenance of storage temperatures (2°C to 8°C) to protect vaccine efficacy.
